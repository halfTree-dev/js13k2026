# Prelude
It wasn't until she woke from a piercing cold that she realized everything around her had lost its color.

The sky was gray. The streets lay black. Strange creatures drifted through the air.

Her gut told her that these monsters had stolen the world’s colors. So she ran toward them.

She fought with everything she had to bring color back to the city. Yet a strange voice echoed in her mind, whispering for her to stop.

She pushed the thought away. All she wanted was to see the rainbow rise again.

# Control
`W` to jump, you can double jump

`S` to accelerate falling

`A` to move left, `D` to move right

`Left Mouse` to shoot.

`P`  to pause.
